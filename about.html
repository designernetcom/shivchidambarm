QUALITY CONTROLS

Incoming Material
  • Visual Check
  • Scraps of one quality are purchased.
  • Scraps generally bought are baled wires, baled/loose sheets, Castings etc.
  • Quality Scraps are PMIed before they are purchased.
  • Spectrometer test for chemical composition
  • Testing of Secondary ingots for quality
  • Raw materials are kept in an enclosure free of moisture.
In process Inspection
  • Temperature Control using pyrometer
  • Degassing & Fluxing
  • Grain refinement where required
  • On line chemical Composition check



Finished Goods
  • Every heat is marked with batch number, which ensure complete back traceability upto raw material stage.
  • Every heat is re-tested for its chemical compositions
  • Each Batch test samples retention for 30 days.
  • Each batch is color coded after the ingots have cooled
  • All manufactured goods are kept in an enclosure free from moisture
